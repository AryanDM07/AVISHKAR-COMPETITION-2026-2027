Everyday parenting

Five habits worth practising

Positive parenting grows through small, repeated actions. Start with what is realistic for your family.

♡
01 · Listen

Listen before reacting

Give your child a chance to explain what happened. Ask questions with curiosity instead of immediately assuming the worst.

☁
02 · Understand

Acknowledge feelings

You can recognise sadness, anger or disappointment without agreeing with every behaviour that follows those emotions.

✿
03 · Encourage

Praise effort and progress

Notice persistence, honesty, improvement and the courage to try again after a mistake.

◎
04 · Guide

Set clear boundaries

Explain reasonable expectations and consequences calmly. Consistency helps children understand what is expected.

☀
05 · Connect

Make time for connection

Share conversations, play and quiet moments together. Let children know that they can come to you when something is worrying them.

↺
A reminder

Repair after difficult moments

If you react harshly, acknowledge it, apologise when appropriate and explain how you will try to respond differently next time.
